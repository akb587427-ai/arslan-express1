GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently
asked questions.

Which areas do you cover?

Arslan Express is planned for Lahore. Share your exact pickup and drop-off areas so availability can be confirmed before booking.

Are prices fixed?

No. Charges depend on distance, item type, urgency, waiting time and any shopping or partner costs. A quote is confirmed with you before the order is accepted.

Can I order a custom cake?

Yes, you can request a cake or gift. Availability, design, size, price and preparation time must be confirmed with the bakery or supplier.

Can I pay online?

Choose JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer as your preference. Payment details are shared after your booking is reviewed. Never send payment to an unverified account.

Is my booking confirmed when I send WhatsApp?

No. It is a request only. Your booking is confirmed after Arslan Express replies and agrees the service, timing and total charges.
