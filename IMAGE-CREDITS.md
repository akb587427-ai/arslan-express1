## Product artwork

The 121 files in `product-images/` are original SVG illustrations generated for this
website. They are not photographs and carry no third-party licence. Every catalogue item
has its own file, so no two products share the same picture.

## Licence notes

- CC0 and public-domain images carry no attribution requirement. Credit is given anyway.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA images require attribution. For CC BY-SA the derivative must keep the
  same licence, so the cropped files in `assets/img/` are offered under the same licence as
  their originals.
- If you replace a photo with your own, delete its row from this table.

## Replacing an image

Category photos: keep the same filename and a 4:3 ratio (for example 900x675).
Product artwork: replace `product-images/item-NNN.svg`, keeping the 720x460 viewBox.
