## Deploy on GitHub + Cloudflare Pages
1. Extract this ZIP on your Android phone.
2. Upload all files inside this folder to the ROOT of your GitHub repository. Do not upload only the ZIP.
3. Keep `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `config.js`, `catalogue.js`,
   `owner-profile.png`, and the `product-images/` folder together in the repository root.
4. In Cloudflare Pages, connect the GitHub repository.
5. Framework preset: None. Build command: leave blank. Build output directory: `/`.
6. Deploy. Later GitHub commits trigger a new deployment.

## Important
- Catalogue entries are illustrative sample options, not confirmation of stock, supplier affiliation or brand endorsement.
- Prices and availability are confirmed manually over WhatsApp.
- This starter website does not store orders in a database, process payments, or provide live tracking.
- The website links the customer to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; the customer must press Send.
- Category photos are 230x135 JPEGs. They look sharp in the service cards but soft at
  full width in the catalogue grid. Supply larger originals (about 900x675) when you can.
- `*` Some services depend on applicable rules and availability; confirm before offering.

## Photos

- Service category cards show the seven category photographs supplied with the site.
- Every catalogue item displays the photo for its category.
- The founder portrait appears in the hero and in the About section.
- The 121 SVG illustrations from an earlier version are still in `product-images/` but are
  no longer displayed. See `IMAGE-CREDITS.md`.

## Multi-item shopping cart

- Customers can add multiple catalogue items to one cart.
- The cart supports quantity increase, decrease and item removal.
- 'Send Cart to WhatsApp' creates one message containing every selected item and quantity.
- No online payment and no order database are included. Prices and availability are
  confirmed in WhatsApp.

## Honest limits

- Prices are never shown on the site. Every price question is answered in WhatsApp.
- The cart builds a message; the customer still has to press Send in WhatsApp.
- There is no stock tracking, no live delivery tracking and no payment processing.
- Catalogue entries are illustrative examples, not a stock list.
